**Invited to Serve as Associate Editor for ACM TALLIP**

I am honored to have been invited to serve as an Associate Editor for the ACM Transactions on Asian and Low-Resource Language Information Processing ([TALLIP](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tallip/)). I have accepted the invitation and look forward to officially beginning my duties shortly. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to the advancement of natural language processing research, particularly in expanding access and innovation for low-resource languages. I look forward to collaborating with the editorial board and the broader research community in this new role.
