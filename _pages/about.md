I am an Associate Professor in the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Department at the National Engineering School of Tunis (ENIT) and a 2023–2024 Fulbright Scholar. My research operates at the intersection of Generative AI, Deep Learning, and distributed systems, with recent work focusing on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Agentic AI for automatic code generation. My research contributions have been recognized by Elsevier, SAGE, Springer, and IEEE. As a two-time Visiting Scholar at UC Berkeley, I possess deep ties to the Bay Area innovation ecosystem. Beyond my academic research, my background in tech startup leadership and my role as an official NVIDIA Ambassador enable me to effectively bridge the gap between academic AI scholarship and commercial product development. I actively organize international AI conferences and am a top-ranked educator specializing in Big Data Analytics, Natural Language Processing, and Virtualization.

## Areas of Interest
- Natural Language Processing
- Generative AI (RAG, LLMs)
- Agentic AI
- Distributed Computing
