**Third Place Award at ACM ICMI 2026 Grand Challenges**

I am thrilled to announce that our work, "Hierarchical Behavioral Transformer Modeling and Multimodal Context Fusion," has been ranked third at the 28th ACM International Conference on Multimodal Interaction (ICMI 2026). Our approach was recognized in the Context-Aware Student Engagement Detection ([CASED](https://www.codabench.org/competitions/15221/#/results-tab)) Grand Challenge. This achievement highlights our ongoing commitment to advancing multimodal AI and improving educational technologies.
